# Results Review — 2Q25

## Earnings (2Q25 vs comps)
- Adjusted revenue, including BCC and bulk sales, was VND16,500 billion, up 53% QoQ but down 43% YoY.
- NPATMI reached VND7,550 billion, representing a 181% increase QoQ but a 31% decline YoY.
- Bulk sales finance income of VND5,400 billion was recognized as compensation from a partner.
- Handovers at Royal Island and Ocean Park 2&3 were key contributors to NPATMI.

## Presales & Backlog
- Contracted sales for the quarter were VND32,500 billion, down 8% YoY and 7% QoQ.
- 1H25 contracted sales totaled VND67,500 billion, up 31% YoY.
- Unbilled backlog stood at VND138,200 billion, a 16% increase YoY, with Royal Island and Wonder City each contributing 59%.
- New launches included Golden City and Green City, with a combined 9,510 low-rise units launched in late June 2025.

## Balance Sheet & Leverage
- Cash and equivalents increased by VND27 trillion QoQ, driven by deposits from partners.
- Gross debt was VND90,400 billion, with a net debt to equity ratio of 17.2%.
- The cash balance saw a significant rise due to joint development project deposits.

## One-offs & Corporate
- A one-off compensation income of VND5,400 billion was recorded in Q2 2025, positively impacting results.
- No significant changes to the 2025 NPATMI forecast are anticipated by sell-side analysts.

## Watch items for 2H25
- Monitor the launch and presales progress of new projects, including Green Paradise and Lang Van, expected in H2 2025.
- Track the company's ability to meet its full-year presales guidance of VND150,000-200,000 billion.
- Assess the impact of any further bulk sales or compensation income on financial performance.
- Evaluate the sustainability of cash flow improvements from joint development projects.
