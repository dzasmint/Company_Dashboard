# Vinhomes (VHM) 2Q25 Results - Buy-Side Short Report

## Headline Summary
Vinhomes reported 2Q25 revenue of VND 19.0 trillion, a YoY decline of 33%, with NPAT at VND 8.3 trillion. Despite the revenue drop, presales performance was robust, driven by retail channels. Our stance remains positive, diverging from the sell-side's in-line assessment, as we see potential for Vinhomes to exceed its full-year guidance.

## Earnings Review (2Q25 vs 1Q25 and 2Q24)
- **Revenue:** VND 19.0 trillion, down 33% YoY. Adjusted revenue, including BCC bulk, was VND 25.7 trillion, down 35% YoY.
- **Gross Profit:** VND 4.5 trillion, with a gross margin not disclosed in financial data.
- **NPAT:** VND 8.3 trillion, with significant contributions from project handovers at VH Ocean Park 2 & 3 and Royal Island.
- **One-offs:** Included VND 5.4 trillion compensation income from a partner [Sell-side].
- **Sell-side View:** Results were labeled as in-line [Sell-side – VCI, BSI], but our analysis suggests potential for exceeding guidance due to strong presales and upcoming bulk sales.

## Presales & Sales Pipeline
- **Contracted Sales:** VND 32.5 trillion, down 8% YoY. However, 1H25 contracted sales grew 31% YoY to VND 67.5 trillion.
- **Unbilled Backlog:** VND 138.2 trillion, up 16% YoY, with Wonder City & Royal Island contributing 59%.
- **New Launches:** Vinhomes Golden City and Green City launched with strong initial bookings.
- **Buy-Side View:** Presales are robust, particularly from retail channels, and new projects are performing well, supporting our positive outlook.

## Balance Sheet & Leverage
- **Cash and Equivalents:** VND 17.937 trillion.
- **Gross Debt:** VND 90.351 trillion, with a total debt to total assets ratio of 13.7%.
- **Leverage:** Interest coverage at 7.1x, with a favorable debt maturity profile.
- **Buy-Side View:** Improved financial position due to strong presales, with cash increasing significantly.

## Guidance & Outlook
- **Management Guidance:** Maintained full-year revenue target of VND 180 trillion and NPAT of VND 42 trillion [Management].
- **Buy-Side View:** We believe Vinhomes is likely to exceed guidance, supported by upcoming bulk sales and strong presales momentum.

## Valuation & Recommendation
- **RNAV Discount:** Currently at 20%, with potential for an increase due to new project announcements in Nha Trang and Ha Noi.
- **Buy-Side Stance:** We maintain a positive outlook, with potential for RNAV uplift, supporting an Accumulate recommendation.

## Catalysts & Risks
- **Catalysts:** Launch of new mega projects and ongoing sales across existing developments [Management].
- **Risks:** Large debt maturity approaching within 1 year [Sell-side – BSI].
- **Buy-Side View:** We see the launch of Vinhomes Can Gio in 4Q25 as a significant catalyst, with risks mitigated by strong cash flows and presales.
